### Ingredients

- 6 cl Amaretto
- 3 cl Fresh lemon juice
- 2 cl Sugar/simple syrup
- 1 Egg white or 3cl of Aquafaba (e.g. juice of chickpeas) as a vegan alternative

### Preparation

1. Put ingredients in the shaker with ice
2. shake shake shake
3. Pour into glass with ice
4. Lemon or orange as garnish
